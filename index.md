<section class="home-section" aria-labelledby="research-title"><h2 id="research-title">Research</h2><p>My research connects genetic variation with the additional information carried on long DNA molecules.</p><ul class="research-list"><li><strong>Cell-type-resolved variation:</strong> using methylation to connect somatic variants and tandem-repeat changes with cell types.</li><li><strong>Genetics and epigenetics:</strong> combining sequence and methylation to improve haplotype phasing and genome interpretation.</li><li><strong>Population epigenomics:</strong> studying DNA methylation, allele-specific patterns, and their relationships with genetic variation in All of Us.</li><li><strong>Rare-disease analysis:</strong> developing practical workflows for family-based long-read sequencing.</li></ul><p><a href="{{ '/research/' | relative_url }}">Research details</a></p></section>
